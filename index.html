Jagstar Auto Pilot

A private tool that uploads and schedules gaming Shorts made from my own gameplay recordings to my YouTube channel, Jagstar (@jagstargaming).

Watch on YouTube — @jagstargaming

What it does

  • Finds highlight moments in my own gameplay recordings.
  • Turns them into vertical YouTube Shorts with titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Uploads them to my own channel through YouTube API Services and schedules them to publish twice a day.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the Jagstar channel. It is a personal desktop tool, not a public service: there are no sign-ups and no other users. It only requests the youtube.upload permission.
